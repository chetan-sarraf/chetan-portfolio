Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about architectural drafting, BOQ valuation, and interior turnkey execution in Nepal.

How much does interior design cost per sq. ft. in Birgunj, Nepal?

Pricing ranges from Rs. 25–45 per sq. ft. for 2D AutoCAD planning & MEP, Rs. 65–110 for complete 3D realistic rendering, and Rs. 1,600–2,800 per sq. ft. for full turnkey execution.

What is the advantage of hiring a Civil Engineer for interior design?

A Civil Engineer ensures load-bearing partitions, plumbing schematics, and ceiling loads are structurally sound. Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimation prevents contractor overcharging.
